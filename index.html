12 • FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does 3FS provide?

Graphic design, UI/UX design, web development, video editing, digital marketing and content creation. Camera-shooting services are not included.

How do I start a project?

Use Start a Project, select the service, plan and preferred date, then submit the enquiry.

Can I choose a project date?

Yes. The enquiry form includes a preferred project date for your team to review.

Will my enquiry reach the team?

After the live database is configured, new enquiries can appear in the private team website.

Can I pay online?

Yes, after a real payment gateway is connected and verified. The included demo does not process real money.
